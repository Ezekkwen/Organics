Home / Our farm

Plot 14 · Selous Farms

Red soil, clean water, patient farming.

About 80 km south-west of Harare on the A5, between Norton and Chegutu, our farm grows fresh food with soil health and water stewardship at its core.

Why we farm this way

We build fertility with compost, manure and cover crops, and rotate crops to support healthy soil. We do not use synthetic pesticides or fertilisers.

Our own borehole supplies drip irrigation to the growing beds. Beneficial insects and approved bio-controls are part of a careful crop-care approach. We harvest to order, then grade and code produce for traceability.

Frequently asked questions

What does your organic registration mean?

Redsoil Organics is ZOPPA-registered. Ask us for the current registration details and certificate copy before placing a trade order.

How do I open a trade account?

Send your business and contact details through the order form or WhatsApp. We will confirm account terms and any references required.

Can I mix crops in an order?

Yes. You can combine crops and pack sizes, subject to current availability and the minimum order for your buyer type.

What happens if harvest falls short?

Standing orders are prioritised. We will give at least 48 hours’ notice if a shortfall affects a confirmed order.

Is dried chilli available all year?

Yes. Fresh chilli is harvested December to July, and dried chilli is available from store from August to November.

Can you deliver beyond Mashonaland West?

Yes. Bulawayo and other destinations can be arranged by courier on quote. Export terms are FCA Harare or Beitbridge.
